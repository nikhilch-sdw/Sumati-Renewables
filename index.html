Manufacturing Excellence

6-Step Integrated Production Process

From high-purity copper rod selection to automated laser optical sorting, every step is monitored for zero defects.

Real-Time Automated Production Machine Simulation

01

ETP Copper Selection

99.99% Electrolytic Tough Pitch copper rod sourcing ensuring maximum electrical conductivity.

02

Micro Wire Drawing

Multi-pass diamond die drawing for precise flat ribbon or round MBB wire sizing.

03

Continuous Annealing

In-line controlled gas atmosphere annealing achieving ultra-soft yield stress (<80 MPa).

04

Hot-Dip Tin Plating

Uniform continuous tin coating (Sn60Pb40, SnAgCu lead-free) with 10-30µm thickness control.

05

Laser Inspection

Dual-axis laser micrometer monitoring for 100% real-time thickness & width compliance.

06

Precision Spooling

Tension-controlled spooling on DIN160/DIN200 reels with vacuum barrier foil packaging.
