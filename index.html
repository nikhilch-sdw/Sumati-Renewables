About Sumati Renewables

Where Solar Module
Efficiency Begins

Founded in 1981, Sumati Renewables (Sumati Electronics Pvt. Ltd.) has evolved into India's foremost specialized manufacturer of Photovoltaic Interconnect Ribbon, Reflective Busbars, and Multi-Busbar Round Wires.

Operating from our integrated manufacturing hub in Partapur Industrial Estate, Meerut, UP, we utilize 99.99% pure ETP/OFC copper and continuous hot-dip tin plating to deliver ultra-soft yield stress ribbons tailored for PERC, TOPCon, and HJT solar modules.

Pan-India & Global OEM Supply Network

Supplying tier-1 solar module manufacturers across India, Europe, Asia, and the Americas.

Micro-Precision Tolerance Control

In-line laser micrometer thickness tracking ensuring ±0.005mm dimensional precision.

Lead-Free & Custom Alloys

Fully RoHS and REACH compliant coating compositions including Sn60Pb40, Sn96.5Ag3.0Cu0.5, and Low-Temp Sn42Bi58.

Manufacturing Excellence

6-Step Integrated Production Process

From high-purity copper rod selection to automated laser optical sorting, every step is rigorously monitored for zero defects.

01

ETP Copper Selection

99.99% Electrolytic Tough Pitch copper rod sourcing ensuring maximum electrical conductivity.

02

Micro Wire Drawing

Multi-pass diamond die drawing for precise flat ribbon or round MBB wire sizing.

03

Continuous Annealing

In-line controlled gas atmosphere annealing achieving ultra-soft yield stress (<80 MPa).

04

Hot-Dip Tin Plating

Uniform continuous tin coating (Sn60Pb40, SnAgCu lead-free) with 10-30µm thickness control.

05

Laser Inspection

Dual-axis laser micrometer monitoring for 100% real-time thickness & width compliance.

06

Precision Spooling

Tension-controlled spooling on DIN160/DIN200 reels with vacuum barrier foil packaging.
