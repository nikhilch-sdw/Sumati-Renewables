Established 1981 • 45 Years Heritage

Where Solar
Efficiency Begins

Founded in 1981, Sumati Renewables (Sumati Electronics Pvt. Ltd.) has evolved over four and a half decades into India’s premier manufacturer of high-precision PV Ribbon, Reflective PV Busbar, and Multi-Busbar Wire. Operating from our state-of-the-art integrated plant in Meerut, UP, we power over 100+ Tier-1 solar module manufacturers across India, Europe, and Asia.

45+ Years of Metallurgical Mastery

Continuous copper drawing, precision rolling, and specialized coating expertise since 1981.

5 GW+ Installed Annual Supply Capacity

Supplying high-yield tinned conductors powering utility-scale solar farms globally.

99.99% ETP Copper Purity

Sourced exclusively from electrolytic tough pitch copper rods for zero electrical resistance loss.

ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001 & RoHS Certified

Rigorous thermal cycling, zero-corrosion tin coatings, and international quality adherence.

Our Mission

To engineer ultra-soft, low-yield-stress solar interconnects that maximize photovoltaic conversion efficiency while eliminating cell micro-cracking and thermomechanical degradation in next-generation high-efficiency solar modules.

Our Vision

To scale production capacity to 10 GW by 2027, pioneering optical 3D reflective interconnects, AI laser micrometer inspection, and sustainable zero-emission green metallurgical manufacturing.

45+ Years Heritage

Milestones That Shaped Sumati

Over four decades of precision metallurgical mastery, technological innovation, and commitment to global clean energy advancement.

1981 Foundation

Inception & Plant Foundation in Meerut

Sumati Electronics established in Mohkampur Industrial Area, Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, pioneering high-precision metallurgical copper drawing, continuous annealing, and specialized electronic conductors.

2010 Metallurgy R&D

Silver-Plated Wire Engineering Division

Successfully developed proprietary continuous silver-plated copper conductor lines, meeting high-spec requirements for advanced electronics and early-stage photovoltaic module research.

2018 Solar Entry

Commercial PV Ribbon Line Launch

Officially launched specialized Solar Photovoltaic Ribbon manufacturing with an initial 10 MT/month capacity, providing ultra-low yield stress tinned copper ribbons to domestic solar module assemblers.

2020 50 MT/Mo Scale

High-Speed Process Automation Scaling

Integrated high-speed automated spooling lines, inert gas annealing furnaces, and inline hot-dip tinning, scaling monthly production capacity fivefold to 50 MT/month.

2021 Busbar Matrix

Precision PV Busbar Line Commissioning

Added a dedicated 60 MT/month precision busbar production matrix. Combined monthly plant output reached 110 MT/month, accommodating large-format M10 and G12 solar wafers.

2022 160 MT/Mo Output

Multi-Busbar (MBB) Wire & PV Wire Expansion

Launched round multi-busbar (MBB) wire and tinned copper PV wire divisions with additional 50 MT/month capacity, expanding total facility output to 160 MT/month.

2024 5 GW Milestone

5 GW Global Supply Benchmark

Achieved 5 GW annual installed manufacturing capacity, establishing trusted supply relationships with over 100+ Tier-1 solar module manufacturers across India, Europe, and Asia.

2025 - 2026+ Next-Gen Tech

10 GW Target & 3D Reflective Interconnects

Scaling toward 10 GW annual interconnect capacity with automated AI laser optical sorting systems and commercial roll-out of patented 120° micro-grooved reflective busbars for TOPCon & HJT cells.
