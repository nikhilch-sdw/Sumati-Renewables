About Sumati Renewables

Pioneering Solar Interconnect Excellence

Over four decades of metallurgical mastery and commitment to renewable energy advancement.

Established 1981

Where Solar
Efficiency Begins

Sumati Renewables (Sumati Electronics Pvt. Ltd.) is India’s premier manufacturer of high-precision PV Ribbon, Reflective PV Busbar, and Multi-Busbar Wire. Operating from an integrated facility in Meerut, UP, we supply over 100+ global solar module manufacturers across India, Europe, and Asia.

5 GW+ Supply Capacity

Supplying high-yield tinned conductors powering gigawatt-scale solar farms.

99.99% ETP Copper Purity

Sourced exclusively from electrolytic tough pitch copper rods for zero electrical resistance loss.

ISO 9001 & RoHS Certified

Compliant with international thermal cycling and corrosion safety standards.

Our Mission

To engineer ultra-soft, low-resistance solar interconnects that maximize power conversion efficiency while eliminating mechanical stress and cell micro-cracking in next-generation solar modules.

Our Vision

To double our production output to 10 GW capacity by 2027 through AI optical inspection and automated green metallurgical manufacturing processes.

45+ Years Heritage

Milestones That Shaped Sumati

Over four decades of metallurgical mastery and commitment to renewable energy advancement.

1982

Foundation in Meerut

Sumati Electronics established with a focus on high-precision electronic components.

2010

Silver Wire Line

Entered silver-plated copper wire manufacturing for electrical and solar sectors.

2018

PV Ribbon Launched

Started PV Ribbon production with a 10 MT/month capacity in solar interconnects.

2020

Capacity Scaling

Enhanced capacity to 50 MT/month with advanced process automation equipment.

2021

Busbar Production

Added 60 MT/month Busbar line; combined output reached 110 MT/month.

2022

PV Wire Division

Launched PV Wire production with 50 MT/month capacity; total output grew to 160 MT/month.

2024

5 GW Expansion

Achieved total installed capacity of 5 GW, serving leading solar module makers.

2025 - 2026+

10 GW Target

Scaling up to 10 GW capacity with AI optical quality sorting systems.

Sumati is a trusted leader in PV Ribbon, PV Wire, and PV Busbar solutions, delivering high-precision, reliable, and performance-driven products for modern solar modules.
