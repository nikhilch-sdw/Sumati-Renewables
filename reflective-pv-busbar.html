Optical Technology

Detailed Product Description

Prismatic light redirection converting busbar shading loss into active module power output gain.

120° Prismatic Micro-Groove Optical Surface Physics

Sumati Reflective PV Busbar features a engineered surface pattern embossed with 120° precision optical triangular micro-grooves. In standard solar modules, sunlight hitting smooth flat copper busbars reflects straight back out through the front glass cover, resulting in wasted optical shading loss.

Our reflective busbar redirects incident light at an oblique angle toward the inner cover glass surface. Because the light strikes the glass-air boundary at an angle greater than the critical angle, Total Internal Reflection (TIR) occurs, trapping the light inside the module encapsulant.

The redirected photons are bounced directly down onto the active silicon solar cell substrate surrounding the busbar, increasing overall module power output by +1.2% to +1.8% while maintaining 100% compatibility with standard automated stringing machines.
