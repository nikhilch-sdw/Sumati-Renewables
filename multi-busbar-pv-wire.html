Detailed Product Description

Ultra-fine round tinned copper wire engineered for 9BB to 16BB next-generation solar module stringing.

Micro Round Wire Geometry & Optical Shading Reduction

As solar cell technology advances to Multi-Busbar (MBB) configurations—such as 9BB, 12BB, and 16BB—conductors must shrink in size while maintaining high current transfer capability. Sumati MBB Wire is a micro round tinned copper wire manufactured with extreme dimensional control (diameters from 0.22 mm to 0.40 mm with ±0.003 mm precision).

The cylindrical profile of our MBB wire scatters incident sunlight sideways into the encapsulant and cover glass, drastically reducing frontal optical shading compared to traditional flat ribbons.

With high roundness accuracy (< 0.002 mm) and ultra-soft elongation (≥ 25%), Sumati MBB wire delivers minimal mechanical stress on delicate N-Type TOPCon, PERC, and Heterojunction (HJT) silicon wafers during high-speed automated soldering.
