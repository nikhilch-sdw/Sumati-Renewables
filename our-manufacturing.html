Our Manufacturing Excellence

Fully automated production lines in Partapur, Meerut equipped with dual-axis laser optical inspection.

State-Of-The-Art Plant

Precision Metallurgical
Process Control

Our Partapur, Meerut manufacturing plant integrates multi-pass diamond die wire drawing, continuous inert gas annealing, and high-speed hot-dip solder tinning to produce PV Ribbon with ±0.005mm dimensional accuracy.

Dual-Axis Laser Control

In-line laser micrometers continuous monitoring width and thickness 1000x per second.

Inert Atmosphere Annealing

Atmospheric gas-controlled furnace producing ultra-soft ribbon (<80 MPa yield stress).

Step-by-Step Workflow

6-Step Integrated Production Process

Monitored for zero defects at every stage of production.

01

ETP Copper Selection

99.99% Electrolytic Tough Pitch copper rod sourcing ensuring maximum electrical conductivity.

02

Micro Wire Drawing

Multi-pass diamond die drawing for precise flat ribbon or round MBB wire sizing.

03

Continuous Annealing

In-line controlled gas atmosphere annealing achieving ultra-soft yield stress (<80 MPa).

04

Hot-Dip Tin Plating

Uniform continuous tin coating (Sn60Pb40, SnAgCu lead-free) with 10-30µm thickness control.

05

Laser Inspection

Dual-axis laser micrometer monitoring for 100% real-time thickness & width compliance.

06

Precision Spooling

Tension-controlled spooling on DIN160/DIN200 reels with vacuum barrier foil packaging.
