Detailed Product Description

Sub-100 micron tinned copper ribbon engineered for ultra-thin silicon wafers and glass-glass solar module architectures.

Sub-100 Micron Ribbon Profile & 98% Cell Mechanical Stress Reduction

Next-generation TOPCon (Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact) and Heterojunction (HJT) solar modules employ thinner silicon wafers (< 130 µm thickness) that are highly sensitive to thermal shock and mechanical stress. Sumati Ultrathin PV Busbar is specially developed with ultra-thin profiles ranging from 60 µm to 95 µm (± 0.003 mm), reducing soldering stress on delicate silicon cells by up to 98%.

Combined with an ultra-soft annealing state (< 75 MPa yield strength), our ultrathin ribbon allows glass-glass and bifacial solar modules to withstand severe thermal cycling without micro-fissure expansion or cell degradation.

Plated with low-temperature solder alloys (such as Sn-Bi and Sn-Ag-Cu), Sumati Ultrathin Busbars enable lower soldering temperature profiles, reducing thermal expansion mismatch between copper and thin silicon wafers.

Sumati is a trusted leader in PV Ribbon, PV Wire, and PV Busbar solutions, delivering high-precision, reliable, and performance-driven products for modern solar modules.
