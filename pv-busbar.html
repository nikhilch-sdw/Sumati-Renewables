Detailed Product Description

Zero-burr tinned flat copper busbar engineered for high current collection and string array interconnections.

Zero-Burr Precision Edge Quality & High Current Load Handling

Sumati Precision PV Busbars are engineered for heavy-duty main string connections and collector leads in high-wattage 550W+ to 700W+ solar modules. Manufactured from 99.99% ETP copper coils and slit using precision rotary blades, our busbars feature 100% burr-free edge profiles, completely eliminating the risk of puncturing EVA encapsulant sheets during vacuum lamination.

Available in custom widths from 4.0 mm to 12.0 mm and thicknesses from 0.20 mm to 0.50 mm, Sumati Busbars carry high operating currents (up to 35 A) with minimal resistive heat generation.

Our continuous hot-dip tinning process applies a 10–25 µm solder coating that provides permanent protection against atmospheric oxidation while guaranteeing strong mechanical solder joint shear strength over 25+ years.
