Detailed Product Description

Metallurgical excellence engineered for automated tabber-stringer lines, high solar cell yield, and long-term module reliability.

High-Purity 99.99% ETP Copper Interconnect Performance

Sumati PV Ribbon is manufactured from 99.99% pure Electrolytic Tough Pitch (ETP) copper rods (TU1/C11000 grade). Through automated precision diamond-die wire drawing and in-line continuous bright annealing under a protective gas atmosphere, our ribbons achieve an ultra-soft yield strength below 80 MPa with elongation greater than 25%.

This ultra-low yield stress minimizes residual mechanical strain during string soldering, virtually eliminating silicon wafer cell bowing and micro-cracking during automated module assembly.

Our continuous hot-dip tinning technology applies a uniform solder coating (10–30 µm per side) with strict layer thickness tolerances. We supply both leaded (Sn60Pb40) and lead-free (Sn96.5Ag3.0Cu0.5 / Sn-Bi) alloys fully compliant with global RoHS and REACH environmental directives.

Sumati is a trusted leader in PV Ribbon, PV Wire, and PV Busbar solutions, delivering high-precision, reliable, and performance-driven products for modern solar modules.
