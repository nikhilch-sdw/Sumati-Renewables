Detailed Product Description

Flexible multi-strand tinned copper lead wire engineered for string jumpers and solar array junction box connections.

Flexible Stranded Conductor & Heavy-Duty Environmental Rating

Sumati PV Wire is designed for internal string-to-string jumper leads, cross-connect interconnects, and junction box terminal wiring in commercial and utility-scale solar PV modules. Built using multi-strand high-purity tinned copper conductors, our wires offer superior flexibility and vibration damping while carrying high DC current loads without thermal resistance buildup.

Featuring cross-linked thermal insulation rated from -40°C up to +120°C, Sumati PV wires exhibit extreme resistance against UV radiation, ozone degradation, salt-mist corrosion, and chemical acid exposure.

With solder zero-cross wetting times under 1.0 second, our PV wires allow solar module assembly plants to achieve fast, high-integrity automated or manual soldering joints during final module lamination.
